### 1. Core Diagnosis & Diagnostic Findings

* **What Happened?**  
  The reported **11% performance improvement is false**. Reconciling all 25,500 payment records reveals that actual cash recovery contracted by **-1.27%** between January (₹16.38 Cr) and July 2026 (₹16.18 Cr). 

* **Why Did It Happen?**  
  Reporting ledgers suffered from critical telemetry errors:
  1. **Webhook Ingestion Duplicates:** Gateway retries generated 2,530 duplicate payment records, overstating collection ledgers by **₹18.58 Cr** (+16.8% overreporting).
  2. **Denominator Manipulation:** 8,583 written-off defaulted accounts were purged from active denominators, artificially inflating recovery rate metrics.
  3. **Attribution Errors:** Infinite lookback logic falsely credited **₹39.2 Cr** of organic borrower repayments to expired calls.

* **How Confident Are We?**  
  **100% Deterministic Confidence.** Primary key deduplication across all transaction logs reconciles exact clean ledger cash with bank statements.
